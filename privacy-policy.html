Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Meridian International Education and Skills Trust ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or make a donation.

1. Information We Collect

When you make a donation or contact us, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Payment transaction details (processed securely via our payment gateway)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and verify your donations securely.
  • Send you receipts and confirmation emails.

  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use third-party services like Razorpay for secure payments, which may use cookies to process transactions and prevent fraud. By continuing to use our site and initiating a payment, you consent to the use of these essential functional cookies.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk of the personal information we process. All direct payment gateways adhere to the standards set by PCI-DSS as managed by the PCI Security Standards Council.
