Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Meridian International Education and Skills Trust appreciates your generous support. Because we are a charitable organization, we have specific policies regarding the cancellation and refund of donations made through our platform.

1. General Policy

Donations made to Meridian Trust are generally considered non-refundable. When you make a donation, the funds are immediately allocated towards our educational, skill-building, and community welfare programs.

2. Technical Errors & Duplicate Charges

We understand that mistakes can happen. We will issue a full refund in the following exceptional circumstances:

  • If an error on our website or payment gateway resulted in your card being charged multiple times for the same transaction.
  • If the donation amount entered was incorrect due to a technical glitch.
  • If your payment method was used fraudulently without your authorization.

3. How to Request a Refund

If you believe you are entitled to a refund based on the criteria above, you must contact us within 7 days of the transaction date.

Please email your refund request to support@meridiantrust.org with the following details:

  • Your full name and phone number.
  • The email address used to make the donation.
  • The exact date and amount of the donation.
  • The Transaction ID / Order ID provided by Razorpay (if available).
  • The reason for your refund request.

4. Processing Time

Once we receive your refund request, our team will review the claim. If approved, the refund will be processed and credited back to the original source of payment (credit card, debit card, or UPI) within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank's processing times.

5. Cancellations for Recurring Donations

If you have set up a recurring (monthly/annual) donation, you may cancel it at any time by contacting us. While future charges will be cancelled immediately, past charges cannot be refunded unless they fall under the "Technical Errors" clause above.
